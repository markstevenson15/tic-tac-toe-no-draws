Game Rules & Variant Rationale

1. The Core Invariants

This variant satisfies two mathematical conditions:

  • No Draw: There is no terminal state in which neither player has won.
  • Always Terminates: Every game terminates in at most 9 moves.

2. Standard 3×3 Grid & 8 Lines

Players alternate turns placing X and O into empty cells. The first player to form 3-in-a-row horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins immediately.

3. The Center Tie-Breaker Rule

If all 9 cells are filled and neither player has achieved 3-in-a-row (a classic "Cat's Game"), the player holding the Center Square (row 2, column 2 / array index [1, 1]) Anchor is declared the winner immediately.

4. Why a Draw Is Impossible

When all 9 squares are full, the center square cannot be empty and cannot be shared. Exactly one player owns it. Therefore, every terminal board state yields a definitive winner.
